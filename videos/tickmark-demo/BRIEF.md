## Intent

Create a polished, narrated hackathon demo that shows Tickmark as a real internal
finance operations product—not a concept deck. Lead with the month-end close
problem, then demonstrate the control tower, deterministic Rulebook, reconciliation,
human review for exceptions, and audit binder. Finish with measured before/after
results and explicit AO build-session evidence. Use the product's own captured screens
as the visual source of truth, with restrained motion and concise annotations.

## Assets

- https://tickmark-kappa.vercel.app — deployed Tickmark product and primary visual source.
- Repository documentation and verified measurement report — source for claims and narration.
- AO session inventory — on-screen proof that AO was used throughout the hackathon.

## Customizations

- Capture the landing page, close control tower, Rulebook, reconciliation, journal entries, run view, and binder.
- Use count-up treatments for 0% to 39% auto-clear, 86 to 71 exceptions, 7 to 6 model calls, and 63 to 47 controller minutes.
- Make deterministic rules visibly distinct from review-only model suggestions.
- Include a clear human-review exception beat and evidence/audit trail beat.
- Show AO session count and representative session names in the final proof section.
- Add professional male narration, subtle modern technology music, captions, and restrained interface-focused motion.
