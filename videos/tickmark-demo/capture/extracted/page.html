Why it gets cheaper

Learning that compiles, not learning that accumulates.

Most agents “learn” by growing a prompt. Every lesson makes the next call longer, slower and more expensive, and none of it can be audited.

Tickmark turns a correction into a rule: a predicate and an action, versioned, readable, and executable with no model call at all. The month the agent learns that AWS codes to 6820 split 60/25/15, that invoice stops costing anything to decide - forever.

So the cost curve bends the right way. More learning means fewer calls, not bigger ones. And a controller can read the rule, disable it, or ask which corrections taught it - which is the difference between automation an auditor signs off and automation they refuse.

The loop

Five steps, and a human owns one of them.

  1. 01
    Rulebook

    the weights - deterministic rules the agent has already earned

  2. 02
    Close run

    the forward pass - rules first, model only on the residue

  3. 03
    Corrections

    the loss signal - what the controller changed, captured structurally

  4. 04
    Gradient step

    distil corrections into candidate rules, backtested against every closed period

  5. 05
    The human gate

    a controller accepts or rejects. Analysis never writes.

Two constraints are borrowed, with credit, from backpass: a rule needs evidence from at least two distinct corrections, and analysis never writes. In accounting those are not niceties - they are traceability and segregation of duties.

What it actually does

Three steps of the close, end to end.

Bank reconciliation

Ties the statement to the ledger across all four cardinalities - one payment, an ACH batch against six invoices, an invoice settled in two tranches, a lockbox deposit. Names every residual as fx, fee, partial or timing, or refuses the match.

AP invoice coding

GL account, department, and class per invoice - including the split allocations that are pure tribal knowledge, like cloud spend carved 60/25/15 across Engineering, Data and Product.

Accrual completeness

Finds the recurring cost that was incurred but never invoiced before cut-off, sizes it off trailing actuals, and books it as a balanced entry with a preparer and an approver who are not the same person.

Guardrails

The parts that make it usable on real books.

Materiality is a hard gate

Nothing above the threshold auto-posts, whatever the confidence. A $12 bank fee and a $2.4M wire do not get the same scrutiny.

Debits equal credits

Checked arithmetically in the database, never taken from model output.

Preparer ≠ approver

Enforced by a constraint, not a convention.

Tickmarks are append-only

You supersede one with a new tickmark. You never edit or delete the record of a verification.

No rule without a backtest

A proposed rule is replayed against every closed period, and the controller sees what it would have broken before adopting it.

Every decision traces

To a rule and its corrections, or to a close run and its evidence chain. No orphan decisions.
